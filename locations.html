What we look for

  • Consistent employee, resident, guest or visitor traffic
  • A secure and accessible indoor placement area
  • Reliable electrical service and suitable floor space
  • Limited or inconvenient nearby food options

What we handle

  • Placement planning and professional installation
  • Product selection, stocking and inventory monitoring
  • Routine cleaning, maintenance and responsive service
  • Ongoing adjustments based on location demand

Strong location candidates

Every property is different. We review traffic, operating hours, space, access and the needs of the people who use the building.

🏢

Offices

Give employees an easy breakfast, lunch, snack and beverage option without adding work for your staff.

🏠

Apartment Communities

Add a useful 24/7 resident amenity in a lobby, clubhouse or shared community space.

🏨

Hotels

Offer guests snacks, drinks, and even travel-convenience items after restaurant and shop hours—without adding work for your front desk.

🏭

Warehouses

Support teams working long, overnight or rotating shifts where nearby food options may be limited.

🏋️

Gyms & Recreation

Offer cold drinks, protein snacks and convenient recovery options close to members.

🏫

Schools

Offer healthy snacks and drinks approved by nutritionists and coaches.

🏥

Healthcare & Other Sites

Provide dependable self-service access for staff, visitors and guests throughout the day.

🚗

Car Dealerships & Auto Service Centers

Offer customers convenient snacks and drinks while they wait for vehicle sales, maintenance or repairs.
